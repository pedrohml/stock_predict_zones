# The ticker universe of the long setup backtest (backtest_entries.py --universe backtest_universe.txt).
#
# Format: '#' starts a comment; one 'version: N' line; '[group] reason' starts a group; other lines
# list Yahoo symbols separated by spaces. TICKER@FIRST..LAST (dates YYYY-MM-DD, either side may be left
# out) uses that symbol's daily rows in that range only, because the others are not usable: broken early
# prices (rows before FIRST count as no history, so its first cases come once a live run's longest
# series fits after FIRST) or sub-penny quotes after a failure (a setup still open at LAST is closed at
# that close). A ticker may be in more than one group. Raise the version whenever a ticker is added,
# removed or cut differently, and name the version with any result made from this file.
#
# Survivor bias is smaller than with today's names alone, not gone. Yahoo serves no history for most
# symbols that were delisted, acquired or renamed away: tried on 19 Nov 2026 and not served were
# Lehman (LEHMQ), Washington Mutual (WAMUQ), old GM (GMGMQ), AMR (AAMRQ), J.C. Penney (JCPNQ), Rite Aid
# (RADCQ), Yellow (YELLQ), Sears (SHLDQ), SVB (SIVBQ), Bed Bath & Beyond (BBBYQ), Credit Suisse (CS),
# Chesapeake (CHK), Walgreens (WBA), US Steel (X), Nordstrom (JWN), Foot Locker (FL), Hanesbrands (HBI),
# DISH, Activision (ATVI), Twitter (TWTR); on B3 OGX (OGXP3), MMX (MMXM3), Gol (GOLL4), Azul (AZUL4,
# before 2026), Cielo (CIEL3), Natura &Co (NTCO3), BRF (BRFS3), JBS (JBSS3), Marfrig (MRFG3), Saraiva
# (SLED4), Eletrobras PNB (ELET6). Renamed symbols that Yahoo serves with their whole history are used
# under the new name (EMBJ3 for EMBR3, AXIA3 for ELET3, BHIA3 for VIIA3). The names that fell hard
# below are the ones that are still quoted, so the worst outcomes (a listing that ends at zero) are
# mostly missing, and every ticker here was picked in 2026, knowing how it did.
#
# Data checks (a day-over-day close ratio above 1.6 or below 1/1.6 since 1995, looked at one by one).
# Left out, because Yahoo's rows have unadjusted splits or adjusted prices thousands of times today's:
# LREN3, CMIG4, GGBR4, GOAU4, BRKM5, GFSA3, OIBR3, CPFE3, PCAR3 (three +110-140% days that look like
# wrong spin-off adjustments), RAPT4 (a recent unadjusted jump), NATU3 (a five-year gap), PARA (history
# from 2021 only, broken prices), AZUL3 (2026 only), KODK (from 2013 only). Their exclusion is about the
# data, not the outcome, but some (GFSA3, OIBR3, BRKM5, PCAR3) were large losers, so it adds to the
# survivor bias. Cut with @: SUZB3 (flat 0.80 then 16.00 in May 2007), RADL3 (a x4.7 day in 2000 and
# x1.7 in 2006), BHIA3 (x0.23 and x2.5 in 2003), COGN3 (x1.8 in 2012), CYRE3 (x1.7 in 2007), B3SA3 (x0.34
# in May 2011), AMER3 (x0.42 and x2.8 around its 2024 reverse split; the 2023 collapse is kept), FRCB and
# BIGGQ (sub-penny quotes after their failures; the collapses are kept), PDGR3 (eleven split-like jumps
# from Oct 2021). Kept as real moves: AAPL Sep 2000, the 2008-2009 days of C, AIG, FNMA, FMCC, GNW, MBI's
# Dec 2023 special dividend (prices are not dividend-adjusted), LUMN Aug 2024, the Ibovespa's -14% day of
# 26 Oct 2026 and the B3 names that fell with it, XRX Oct 2026. BBDC4 has an unexplained x0.57 day on
# 29 May 2009; it is kept, uncut, because it is one of the original twelve.
