  Rules:
  - The setups are made after the as-of day's close; the walk starts with the next daily candle
    (that day's own candle is never used) and ends with the candle of valid_until, the horizon's
    last close. Daily candles are used whatever the horizon's close basis.
  - Fill: a limit order fills when the candle reaches its entry (a long's low at or below it, a
    short's high at or above it); a stop order fills when the candle reaches its entry in the
    order's direction (a long's high at or above it, a short's low at or below it). A touch is
    enough. When the open is already past the entry, the order fills at the open (better than the
    entry for a limit, worse for a stop). A market order fills at the next candle's open, not at the
    as-of close it was priced at. An unfilled order stays in place until valid_until, even if the
    price runs to the target first.
  - After the fill: the stop or the target closes the setup at its price; an open past the stop or
    the target closes it at that open (a gap through the stop can lose more than 1R). Still open
    after the last candle: closed at that candle's close (expiry).
  - Ordering inside a candle is unknown. On the fill candle, a level the price must have crossed
    after the entry (the entry lies between the open and the level) is hit after the fill; any other
    level the candle reaches may have come before it. When the candle allows more than one result
    (stop or target, stop or still open, target or still open), the default is the pessimistic one
    (stop first, target not yet); --optimistic takes the other. Each setup is played both ways, and
    'ambiguous' counts those whose result differs.
  - R = (exit - fill price) / risk for a long, (fill price - exit) / risk for a short, where risk is
    the setup's own entry-to-stop distance. An order that never fills is 0R. A win is an exit at the
    target.
  Caveats:
  - Mechanical zones and unadjusted probabilities (mechanical.py), as a live `--engine mechanical`
    run makes them: not Claude's zones or adjustments. The inputs carry no events.
  - No commissions, spread, slippage, borrowing costs or dividends; prices are split-adjusted and
    not dividend-adjusted, so a short does not pay the dividend and a long does not receive it.
  - The ordering inside a daily candle is unknown and counted pessimistically by default
    (--optimistic gives the other bound). Touch fills are generous for limit orders.
  - Tickers chosen today are survivors, in what was mostly a rising market for them.
  - Cases overlap whenever the step is shorter than the horizon, the three setups of a case share
    one price path, and tickers move together; the bootstrap resamples whole cases and still
    understates the uncertainty.
  - This checks the calibration of the stated odds of hypothetical setups. It is not a strategy, not
    a claim about returns, and not advice.
